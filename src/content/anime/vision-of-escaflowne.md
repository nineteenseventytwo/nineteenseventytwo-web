Stop me if you heard about the one where a high school student is pulled away from their dull life and pulled into a fantasy world where they are all powerful and guests of honor to the ruling class. Oh yeah and the world is still reeling from some super-weapon I'm sure we all know not to try to use again... Slay a dragon, take its heart, power your mobile suit - wow. An excellent blend of fantasy and mecha worlds. A 24 episode series (there's a film as well I need to get to) where our teenage tarot master protagonist finds herself transported to a world where fate is literally being toyed with by strange powers.
