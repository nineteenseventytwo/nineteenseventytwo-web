Although unfinished worth the watch with beautifully animated mobile suit fight sequences, cute father daughter moments, interesting lore involving awakening identity and ancient systems of power in a mechanized world. The 'Neo Evolution' opening track and guitar solo will be stuck in your head forever. The soul gear outfits are high fashion.
