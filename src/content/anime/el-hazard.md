Stop me if you heard about the one where a high school student is pulled away from their dull life and tossed into a fantasy world where they are all powerful and guests of honor to the ruling class. Oh yeah and the world is still reeling from some super-weapon I'm sure we all know not to try to use again... This 7 part OVA follows our all powerful protagonist teenager across worlds where a struggle between humanity, bugs, and shadowfolk unfolds across glorious fantasy animation. Even includes a drunken master sensei chaperone and a loyal impenetrable cat you can wear as a shield, no NSFW here folks.
