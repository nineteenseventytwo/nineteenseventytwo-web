Ushiwaka is the oldest child born in space and he must pass the space piloting exam - a reasonable expectation of a 12 year old boy. The only issue is that a disaster unfolds during his exam and capturing Haley's comet will have to wait. Excellent hard-science storyline, hand-drawn technical anime, and catchy synth guitar futurewave music. What should we do when 'the company' decides they won't send rescue ships? I guess we won't find out, this anime remains unfinished, but the 2 episodes are fab.
